\chapter*{Abstract}
In this Final Degree Project, the calculation and design of a \SI{20}{\kilo\volt} overhead high-voltage line, with a single circuit and simplex configuration, intended for the evacuation of the energy generated by a \SI{6.5}{\mega\watt} photovoltaic power plant, is developed. The project has been structured following the typical sections of a standard overhead high-voltage line project with a nominal voltage below \SI{36}{\kilo\volt}, so that both the index, the content and the technical documentation comply with the usual criteria for this type of installation.

The route of the line is located in the province of Alicante, between the municipalities of Benidorm and L’Albir, an area characterized by orography and environmental conditions that influence the mechanical, electrical and safety design of the installation. The study includes the definition of the line route in plan and longitudinal profile, the selection of aluminium-steel conductors, supports, cross-arms, insulator strings and protection systems, as well as compliance with regulatory clearances and environmental and safety requirements.

The design of the line has been carried out using the IMEDEXSA software. In parallel, all electrical, mechanical, foundation and earthing calculations have been independently programmed and developed using MATHCAD, following the procedures and criteria established by the applicable regulations, with the aim of verifying the consistency and validity of the results obtained with the design software. This approach allows the comparison of results and enhances the understanding of the calculation process of an overhead high-voltage line.

Electrical calculations are performed, including maximum admissible currents, voltage drop, power losses and line efficiency, as well as mechanical calculations of conductor tensions and sags under different loading hypotheses. In addition, monoblock foundations for the supports are analysed, and the earthing system is designed, verifying compliance with admissible step and touch voltages to ensure personal safety.

Finally, the project is completed with the construction drawings, the technical specifications, the budget and the health and safety study, resulting in a technically feasible solution, compliant with current regulations and representative of a real medium-voltage overhead line project for renewable energy evacuation.
